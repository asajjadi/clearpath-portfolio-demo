# AI-Guided Medical Device Project Management

## The problem
New project managers entering medical-device development must coordinate technical work while learning design controls, risk management, verification, transfer, and submission concepts. Conventional project boards show tasks, but rarely explain the purpose or relationships behind them.

## The concept
ClearPath pairs a project board and lifecycle timeline with contextual guidance and an AI Coach. The concept supports learning, review preparation, and prioritization while keeping expert judgment in control.

## Public-safe demonstration boundary
- This public portfolio demo uses synthetic projects, records, names, metrics, and scenarios.
- It is read-only: no accounts, database, AI provider, credentials, client data, clinical data, or production integration.
- It is not a validated QMS, regulatory submission system, or source of regulatory advice.

## AI governance approach
AI is advisory. A responsible deployment would require authentication, scoped permissions, immutable logging, human approval before any regulated-record change, controlled prompt handling, and validated operating procedures. No AI output should establish compliance, release a product, or make a clinical decision.

## What it demonstrates
- Product definition for a regulated technical workflow
- Full-stack architectural thinking: user experience, backend services, auditability, and configurable AI providers
- Human-governed agent design: read-only insight by default and controlled action scope
- Practical design-control and technical program-management fluency
